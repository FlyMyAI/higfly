# Camera moves - the "presets" Higgsfield sells, as plain prompts

Higgsfield's Cinema Studio is, under the hood, a library of camera-move prompts wrapped in a UI. Here they are as plain text. Prepend one to your shot; edit them, mix them, add your own. No studio, no credits.

Usage: `--preset crash-zoom` prepends the phrasing below to your prompt before it hits the video model.

| Preset | Prepended phrasing |
|---|---|
| `crash-zoom` | Cinematic crash zoom, sudden fast push-in on the subject, dramatic, anamorphic lens flare, 35mm film grain |
| `dolly-zoom` | Cinematic dolly zoom (vertigo effect), the background warps and stretches while the subject stays fixed, unsettling, filmic |
| `crane-up` | Sweeping crane shot rising up and pulling back to reveal the full scene, epic scale, volumetric light |
| `orbit` | Smooth 180-degree orbit around the subject, shallow depth of field, cinematic bokeh, steady motion |
| `tracking` | Steadicam tracking shot following the subject from behind, kinetic, immersive, subtle motion blur |
| `fpv-drone` | Fast FPV drone flythrough weaving past obstacles at high speed, wide-angle GoPro look, motion blur |
| `slow-push` | Slow cinematic push-in, tension building, drifting particles, volumetric god rays, film grain |
| `whip-pan` | Fast whip pan transition into the scene, motion streaks, energetic, music-video pacing |

These are starting points, not magic. The frontier model (Seedance / Veo / Kling) does the heavy lifting; the phrasing just steers the camera. Tune them for your subject and you will beat a locked preset - because you can read and edit it.
